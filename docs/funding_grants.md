## Grant Opportunities

### 1. Karnataka Agri Grand Challenge
- **Amount:** Up to ₹50 lakh
- **Who:** Karnataka Agriculture Department + IIIT-B
- **What they want:** Tech solutions for Karnataka farmers
- **Difficulty:** Medium (200-300 applications, 10-15 funded)
- **Our advantage:** Kannada-native, coastal KA focus — exactly what they want
- **Requirement:** Working prototype + problem statement (we have both)

### 2. NABARD Innovation Fund
- **Amount:** ₹5-25 lakh
- **What they want:** Rural/agri innovation
- **Difficulty:** Easier than VC. Mandated to fund rural tech.
- **How to apply:** Nearest NABARD regional office (Mangalore has one)

### 3. RKVY-RAFTAAR (Best Fit)
- **Amount:** Up to ₹25 lakh + mentorship
- **What they want:** Agri startups specifically
- **Apply at:** rkvy.nic.in
- **Why us:** Working prototype with real farmer focus

### 4. iStart Karnataka (Easiest)
- **Amount:** ₹5-10 lakh
- **What they want:** Karnataka state startups
- **Difficulty:** Easiest — less competition than national grants
- **Apply at:** istart.karnataka.gov.in
- **Why us:** Kannada-native is a huge plus

### 5. Startup India Seed Fund
- **Amount:** ₹20-25 lakh
- **Difficulty:** Medium — needs DPIIT registration
- **Requirement:** Registered startup

### 6. Atal Innovation Mission
- **Amount:** ₹10-25 lakh
- **Difficulty:** Medium — needs incubator affiliation

---

## Pre-requisites Before Applying

### Step 1: Register (do this week)
- Register on Startup India portal (startupindia.gov.in) — free, online
- Get DPIIT recognition number — this unlocks most grants
- May need to register as LLP or Private Limited (₹1,000-5,000 cost)

### Step 2: Get 10-20 farmer users (this week)
- Install APK on phone, share via WhatsApp
- Screenshot their usage, feedback, testimonials
- Key metric: do they open the app on day 2, 3, 7?

### Step 3: Apply to RKVY-RAFTAAR + iStart Karnataka first

## Why Grants Over VC

- No equity given up (company stays 100% yours)
- They fund early stage prototypes (not revenue)
- They WANT to give money (it's their mandate)
- Less competition than VC (most builders ignore grants)

## Timeline
- Paperwork + registration: 1 week
- Farmer testing + testimonials: 1-2 weeks
- Application submission: week 3
- Decision: 2-4 months from submission

## Key Insight
Grants are very gettable for us. The app is EXACTLY what these programs fund.
But we need 10-20 real farmer users first — that turns the application from
"idea" to "validated product."
